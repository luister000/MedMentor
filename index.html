How it works

  1. Quick registration: Residents join via WhatsApp/Telegram or through a simple web form, completing specialty and level (R1–R3).
  2. Case submission: They submit a brief summary of a real clinical case. The AI summarizes the text and generates 2–3 guiding questions.
  3. Mentor feedback: A certified mentor replies with concise voice or text feedback (≤2 min) including key insights.
  4. AI synthesis: The AI generates a structured summary (question → answer → learning point) stored in the resident’s personal dashboard.
  5. Quick feedback: A 30-sec micro-NPS survey closes the loop, feeding data into the learning analytics panel.
